## About

I am a Brazilian data analyst connecting healthcare operations, business intelligence, machine learning, SQL, cloud analytics and decision-focused communication.

My work starts with messy public or operational data and ends in something people can inspect: dashboards, notebook reports, reproducible pipelines, model comparisons, maps, and portfolio-ready case studies.

The full portfolio is here:

**[luandarodrigues-portfolio.vercel.app](https://luandarodrigues-portfolio.vercel.app/)**

## Current Direction

I am building a portfolio around analytical products that are:

- reproducible enough to audit;
- visual enough to explain;
- practical enough to support decisions;
- careful enough to show uncertainty and limits.

Recent notebook work includes Bayesian pharmacovigilance, causal inference for remote salary differences, crypto market regime detection, and Brazilian inflation/monetary-regime clustering.
