About

From engineering and healthcare to data-driven decision-making.

My path started in Biomedical Engineering, where I built a technical foundation for understanding systems, processes and the relationship between technology and healthcare. Over time, that interest moved naturally toward data: how information is structured, how indicators explain operations, and how analysis can support better decisions.

I later strengthened this trajectory through Hospital Management and entered the public healthcare environment through a competitive EBSERH selection process, achieving 5th place. At HC-UFU, I work with clinical pharmacy and dispensing operations, KPI monitoring, process analysis, bottleneck identification and decision support in a complex hospital setting.

Before consolidating this path in healthcare operations and data, I also worked across marketing, scientific communication and digital projects at Indigo Hive. That experience added a practical layer in content creation, HTML email marketing, design thinking, automation, AI-supported workflows and communication for pharmaceutical and health technology contexts.

My career has been built through constant learning: Python, SQL foundations, Power BI, Copilot, AWS foundations, Lean Six Sigma and continuous technical development. The common thread has always been the same: using data, technology and communication to make complex work clearer, more measurable and more useful.
